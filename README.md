# Physical Sciences Machine Shop at UCLA

Welcome to the central hub for precision fabrication and design within the UCLA Division of Physical Sciences.

## Our Services
* **CNC Machining:** High-tolerance milling and turning.
* **Manual Machining:** Rapid prototyping and repairs.
* **Water Jet and Wire EDM cutting:** High precision cutting ahd processing
* **3D Printing:** Rapid prototyping using professional grade 3D printers.
* **Consultation/Training:** Expert advice and training on material selection, part design, and specific machine use.
